Our Story

Four generations of authentic Italian cooking

The Beginning

Mamma Mia Ristorante was founded in 2007 by Nonna Cupi in the heart of Naples, Italy. What started as a small family trattoria with only 4 tables, serving handmade pasta to local workers, quickly became a neighborhood favorite. Cupi's philosophy was simple: use only the freshest ingredients, make everything from scratch, and serve every dish with love.

In 2010, Cupi's husband Japarah moved the business to a bigger city and opened the first Mamma Mia outside Italy. The restaurant grew, but the recipes remained exactly the same — handwritten notes from Nonna's kitchen.

Today, Mamma Mia has locations in many cities, but we still make our pasta fresh every morning. Every sauce is simmered for hours. Every pizza is wood-fired. Because that's how Nonna taught us.

Our Philosophy

"La buona cucina è fatta di ingredienti semplici e tanto amore."

— Good cooking is made of simple ingredients and lots of love.

We believe food brings people together. Every dish we serve tells a story of tradition, family, and passion. No shortcuts. No frozen ingredients. Just authentic Italian cooking, passed down for generations.
